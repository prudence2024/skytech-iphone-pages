Do you sell brand new iPhones or only used?

Both. Brand new sealed units and UK-sourced used devices are listed in the same catalogue, clearly marked by condition. Filter by condition on the Discover section above.

How do I confirm stock and final pricing?

Tap any WhatsApp enquiry button. A Skytech team member confirms live stock, condition specifics and final pricing in chat — usually within hours.

What does "UK Used" actually mean?

A genuine iPhone sourced from the UK market, previously owned and returned to working condition. Each unit is inspected in-house and its battery health and cosmetic grade are disclosed before purchase.

Do you handle repairs for iPhones not bought from you?

Yes. The repair workshop services all iPhone generations regardless of where the device was purchased. Submit the repair enquiry form above and we'll respond on WhatsApp.

Do you offer delivery outside Lagos?

Nationwide delivery is available. Shipping arrangements and cost are confirmed on WhatsApp per order, based on your location and the device.
